6.1 Polynomials with coefficients in ℝ (系数在实数域上的多项式)

Core Definition (核心定义)

A polynomial with coefficients in \mathbb{R} (the set of real numbers) is an expression of the form:
 $P(x) = a_nx^n + a_{n-1}x^{n-1} + \dots + a_1x + a_0 $
• x is the indeterminate (不定元) – a placeholder for real numbers.

•$ a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n \in \mathbb{R} $are the coefficients (系数).

• $a_n \neq 0 (if n \geq 1) $is the leading coefficient (首项系数), and n is the degree (次数) of P(x), denoted \deg(P).

•$ a_0 $is the constant term (常数项).

中文讲解与翻译

• 多项式的本质是“含不定元的有限次幂的线性组合”，系数均为实数。

• 术语翻译：indeterminate (不定元)、coefficients (系数)、leading coefficient (首项系数)、degree (次数)、constant term (常数项)。

• 例：$P(x) = 3x^2 - 2x + 5 $是二次多项式（degree 2），首项系数$ a_2=3$，常数项 $a_0=5$。

What is P(a+bx)? (什么是P(a+bx)？)

P(a+bx) is the composition (复合) of the polynomial P with the linear expression a+bx (where a, b \in \mathbb{R}). It means replacing every occurrence of x in P(x) with a+bx.

Step-by-Step Example (分步示例)

Let P(x) = 2x^2 + 3x - 1, a=1, b=2 (so a+bx = 1+2x):

1. Replace x with 1+2x in P(x):
 P(1+2x) = 2(1+2x)^2 + 3(1+2x) - 1 
2. Expand and simplify:

\begin{align*}
2(1+4x+4x^2) + 3+6x - 1 &= 2+8x+8x^2 + 3+6x - 1 \\
&= 8x^2 + 14x + 4
\end{align*}

中文讲解与翻译

• P(a+bx) 表示“将多项式P中的不定元x替换为线性表达式a+bx”，属于多项式的复合运算。

• 术语翻译：composition (复合)、linear expression (线性表达式)、replace (替换)、expand and simplify (展开并化简)。

• 关键：替换时需将所有x统一替换，不可遗漏项（如常数项无需替换，仅含x的项替换）。

Basic Operations on Polynomials (多项式的基本运算)

1. Addition (加法)
For P(x) = \sum_{k=0}^n a_kx^k and Q(x) = \sum_{k=0}^m b_kx^k (let l = \max(n,m)):
 (P+Q)(x) = \sum_{k=0}^l (a_k + b_k)x^k 
(Set a_k=0 if k>n, b_k=0 if k>m)

2. Multiplication (乘法)
 (P \cdot Q)(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n+m} \left( \sum_{i+j=k} a_i b_j \right)x^k 
中文讲解与翻译

• 加法：同次幂的系数相加，次数取两个多项式的最高次。

• 乘法：交叉相乘所有项后合并同类项，次数为两个多项式次数之和。

• 术语翻译：addition (加法)、multiplication (乘法)、combine like terms (合并同类项)。

• 例：P(x)=x+2, Q(x)=3x-1，则P(x)\cdot Q(x)=3x^2 +5x -2。

6.4 Irreducibility in K[x] (多项式环K[x]中的不可约性)

Background: Polynomial Ring K[x] (背景：多项式环K[x])

Let K be a field (域，e.g., \mathbb{R} for real numbers, \mathbb{Q} for rationals). K[x] denotes the set of all polynomials with coefficients in K, equipped with polynomial addition and multiplication.

中文讲解与翻译

• 域（field）是满足“加、减、乘、除（非零元）封闭”的代数结构（如实数域\mathbb{R}、有理数域\mathbb{Q}）。

• K[x] 称为“域K上的多项式环”，是所有系数在K中的多项式构成的集合，配备多项式加法和乘法。

• 术语翻译：field (域)、polynomial ring (多项式环)、equipped with (配备)。

Definition of Irreducibility (不可约的定义)

A non-constant polynomial f \in K[x] is irreducible (不可约) over K if it cannot be written as a product of two non-constant polynomials g, h \in K[x]. If it can be factored this way, f is reducible (可约).

Key Notes (关键要点)

• Irreducibility depends on K (不可约性依赖于域K):

◦ E.g., f(x) = x^2 - 2 is irreducible over \mathbb{Q} (cannot be factored into linear polynomials with rational coefficients), but reducible over \mathbb{R}: f(x) = (x-\sqrt{2})(x+\sqrt{2}).

• Linear polynomials (degree 1) are always irreducible over any field (一次多项式在任何域上均不可约)。

中文讲解与翻译

• 不可约多项式：非零次多项式无法分解为两个非零次多项式的乘积（系数均在域K中）。

• 核心：不可约是“相对概念”，依赖于系数所在的域。同一多项式在某域不可约，在更大的域中可能可约。

• 术语翻译：irreducible (不可约的)、reducible (可约的)、linear polynomial (一次多项式)、rational coefficients (有理系数)。

Criterion for Irreducibility Over \mathbb{Q}: Eisenstein’s Criterion (有理数域上的不可约判别法：艾森斯坦判别法)

Let f(x) = a_nx^n + \dots + a_1x + a_0 \in \mathbb{Z}[x] (polynomial with integer coefficients). If there exists a prime number p such that:

1. p divides a_0, a_1, \dots, a_{n-1} ( p 整除所有非首项系数),

2. p does not divide a_n ( p 不整除首项系数),

3. p^2 does not divide a_0 ( p^2 不整除常数项),

then f(x) is irreducible over \mathbb{Q}.

Example (示例)

Prove f(x) = 2x^3 - 6x + 3 is irreducible over \mathbb{Q}:

• Choose prime p=3:

1. 3 divides a_0=3, a_1=-6 (non-leading coefficients),

2. 3 does not divide a_3=2 (leading coefficient),

3. 3^2=9 does not divide a_0=3.

• By Eisenstein’s Criterion, f(x) is irreducible over \mathbb{Q}.

中文讲解与翻译

• 艾森斯坦判别法是判断有理系数多项式不可约的常用工具，核心是找到一个素数p满足三个条件。

• 术语翻译：Eisenstein’s Criterion (艾森斯坦判别法)、integer coefficients (整数系数)、prime number (素数)、divides (整除)。

• 注意：若多项式系数为有理数，可先乘以公分母化为整系数多项式，再应用判别法。

6.5 Roots of Polynomials & The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra (多项式的根与代数基本定理)

Definition of a Root (根的定义)

A number r \in K (or r \in \mathbb{C} for complex roots) is a root (根) of f(x) \in K[x] if f(r) = 0.

Factor Theorem (因式定理)

r is a root of f(x) if and only if (x - r) is a factor (因式) of f(x), i.e., f(x) = (x - r)g(x) for some g(x) \in K[x].

中文讲解与翻译

• 多项式的根：代入多项式后结果为0的数（可是实数、复数等）。

• 因式定理：根与一次因式一一对应，即“r是根”等价于“(x-r)是因式”。

• 术语翻译：root (根)、factor (因式)、Factor Theorem (因式定理)。

• 例：r=2是f(x)=x^2 -4的根，因为f(2)=0，且f(x)=(x-2)(x+2)。

Multiplicity of a Root (根的重数)

If f(x) = (x - r)^k g(x) where g(r) \neq 0 and k \geq 1, then r is a root of multiplicity k ( k 重根).

中文讲解与翻译

• 重根：若(x-r)^k是多项式的因式，但(x-r)^{k+1}不是，则r是k重根。

• 术语翻译：multiplicity (重数)、k-fold root (k重根)。

• 例：f(x)=(x-1)^2(x+3)中，r=1是2重根，r=-3是1重根（单根）。

The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra (代数基本定理)

Every non-constant polynomial f(x) \in \mathbb{C}[x] (polynomial with complex coefficients) has at least one root in \mathbb{C} (the set of complex numbers).

Corollary (推论)

Every non-constant polynomial f(x) \in \mathbb{C}[x] of degree n can be factored uniquely (up to order of factors) as:
 f(x) = c(x - r_1)(x - r_2)\dots(x - r_n) 
where c \in \mathbb{C} (non-zero constant) and r_1, \dots, r_n \in \mathbb{C} are roots (counting multiplicities).

中文讲解与翻译

• 代数基本定理：每个复系数非零次多项式在复数域中至少有一个根。

• 推论：复系数n次多项式可唯一分解为n个一次因式的乘积（根含重数）。

• 术语翻译：Fundamental Theorem of Algebra (代数基本定理)、complex coefficients (复系数)、counting multiplicities (计重数)、unique factorization (唯一分解)。

• 意义：复数域是“代数闭域”，多项式在其中可完全分解为一次因式，而实数域中不可（如x^2 +1在\mathbb{R}中不可约，在\mathbb{C}中分解为(x-i)(x+i)）。

6.6 The Gauss Lemma and Some Consequences (高斯引理及其推论)

Gauss Lemma (高斯引理)

If a polynomial f(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[x] (integer coefficients) can be factored as f(x) = g(x)h(x) with g(x), h(x) \in \mathbb{Q}[x] (rational coefficients), then it can also be factored as f(x) = G(x)H(x) with G(x), H(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[x], where \deg(G) = \deg(g) and \deg(H) = \deg(h).

中文讲解与翻译

• 高斯引理：整系数多项式若能分解为有理系数多项式的乘积，则必能分解为整系数多项式的乘积（次数不变）。

• 核心：将有理系数分解“还原”为整系数分解，简化有理系数多项式的不可约性判断（可转化为整系数多项式的判断）。

• 术语翻译：Gauss Lemma (高斯引理)、integer coefficients (整系数)、rational coefficients (有理系数)、factor into (分解为)。

Consequence 1: Rational Root Theorem (推论1：有理根定理)

Let f(x) = a_nx^n + \dots + a_1x + a_0 \in \mathbb{Z}[x] with a_n \neq 0 and a_0 \neq 0. If \frac{p}{q} (in lowest terms, p \in \mathbb{Z}, q \in \mathbb{N}) is a rational root of f(x), then p divides a_0 and q divides a_n.

Example (示例)

Find rational roots of f(x) = 2x^3 - 5x^2 + x + 2:

• Possible rational roots: \pm1, \pm2, \pm\frac{1}{2} ( p divides a_0=2, q divides a_3=2 ).

• Test: f(1)=0, f(2)=0, f(-\frac{1}{2})=0 – these are all rational roots.

中文讲解与翻译

• 有理根定理：整系数多项式的有理根\frac{p}{q}（既约分数）满足“分子p整除常数项a_0，分母q整除首项系数a_n”。

• 用途：快速枚举所有可能的有理根，再验证是否为根。

• 术语翻译：Rational Root Theorem (有理根定理)、lowest terms (既约分数)、divides (整除)、enumerate (枚举)。

Consequence 2: Irreducibility Over \mathbb{Q} vs. \mathbb{Z} (推论2：\mathbb{Q}与\mathbb{Z}上的不可约性等价)

A non-constant polynomial f(x) \in \mathbb{Z}[x] is irreducible over \mathbb{Q} if and only if it is irreducible over \mathbb{Z} (i.e., cannot be factored into non-constant polynomials with integer coefficients).

中文讲解与翻译

• 推论：整系数多项式在有理数域上不可约，当且仅当它在整数域上不可约。

• 意义：将有理系数多项式的不可约性判断，完全转化为整系数多项式的不可约性判断（可结合艾森斯坦判别法、有理根定理等）。

• 术语翻译：equivalent (等价的)、if and only if (当且仅当)。

6.8 Exercises (练习要点)

1. Polynomial Composition (多项式复合)：Practice computing P(a+bx) by substitution and expansion (e.g., P(x)=x^4 - 1, a=0, b=2 → P(2x)=16x^4 -1).

2. Irreducibility判断：Use Eisenstein’s Criterion and Rational Root Theorem to determine irreducibility over \mathbb{Q} (e.g., f(x)=3x^5 - 5x^3 + 15x - 10 – test p=5).

3. Root Finding (求根)：Apply Rational Root Theorem to find rational roots, then factor the polynomial (e.g., f(x)=x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6 → roots 1,2,3 → f(x)=(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)).

4. Factorization Over \mathbb{C} (复数域分解)：Use the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra to factor real polynomials into complex linear factors (e.g., f(x)=x^2 + 4 → (x-2i)(x+2i)).
